# Laya Consulting Design Analysis

## Visual Identity
- **Palette**: Pure black (#000000) backgrounds with cyan/teal (#00D4FF) primary accents. Subtle gradients (teal to dark) for depth.
- **Typography**: Likely **Outfit** for headings (modern, clean, geometric) and **Inter** for body text. High weight contrast in headings.
- **Components**:
    - **Cards**: Rounded corners (approx 24-32px), subtle borders (1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)), and glow effects on hover.
    - **Buttons**: Pill-shaped. Primary is solid teal with dark text. Secondary is outlined or "liquid glass" style.
    - **Imagery**: High-quality product mockups (emails/SMS on phones) with floating elements.

## Motion Design (The "Flow")
- **Entrance**: Staggered reveals for almost everything. Headlines usually slide up and fade in.
- **Hover States**: 3D tilt effects or scale-up with glow for cards. Smooth transitions (approx 0.4s ease-out).
- **Scroll Effects**: Parallax on background elements (like the "ellipse" svg). Sections feel connected rather than isolated blocks.
- **Retentivity**: Use of "Read More" and interactive lightboxes to keep users on the page and exploring content.

## Layout Structure
- **Hero**: Minimalist with a strong value proposition and clear CTAs.
- **Benefits**: 3-column grid with icons.
- **Tutorials/Content**: Video grid (YouTube embeds) with custom thumbnails.
- **Process**: Step-by-step with large numbers and descriptive text.
- **FAQ**: Clean accordion with smooth height transitions.

## Better Ways to Arrange RetentionFirm
1. **Problem Section**: Instead of a simple list, use a "Pain Point Grid" with 3D cards that light up on hover.
2. **Solution Section**: Use an immersive background that transitions from the problem section. Add a "Before vs After" visual.
3. **Results Section**: Use a "Bento Grid" layout for different metrics, making them feel like a dashboard.
4. **Process Section**: Use a horizontal scroll or a vertically pinned timeline with 3D step cards.
5. **Animations**: Implement GSAP ScrollTrigger for all section reveals to create that "award-winning" flow.
